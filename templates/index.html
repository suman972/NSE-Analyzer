    🛡️ 4 Golden Trading Rules of Institutional OI

    • 1
      Keep SL in System & Trail: Never trade naked without predefined stop loss in order book. Protect capital before seeking profits.
    • 2
      World Market Trend Alignment: Global cues (GIFT Nifty, US tech indices, crude) should not conflict with direction. Avoid fighting macroeconomic tides.
    • 3
      Watch Impacting News: RBI/Fed announcements or geopolitical news can trigger sudden gamma shocks. Respect high-volatility event windows.
    • 4
      Price Action Confirmation: Trade only when open interest bias confirms on 5m/15m chart levels. Open Interest indicates bias; Price Action triggers entries.

    📖 Quick Reading Guide & Institutional Logic

    Added Longs / Closed Shorts: Bullish institutional activity (Long Buildup / Short Covering)
    Closed Longs / Added Shorts: Bearish institutional activity (Long Unwinding / Short Buildup)
    Bought Net: Net contract accumulation today
    Sold Net: Net contract distribution today
    In-The-Money (ITM) Golden Tint: High intrinsic value contracts with real institutional conviction
    Retail Trap Alert: When Retail Clients take massive one-sided positions opposite to FII & Pro desks
