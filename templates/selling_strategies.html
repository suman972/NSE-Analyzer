Zerodha Varsity Option Selling Framework

Core quantitative principles from Module 5: "Options Theory for Professional Trading"

🎲

1. The Statistical Edge of the Writer

In every option contract, there are 3 possible directional outcomes: the asset rises, stays flat, or drops. While the option buyer needs an immediate, aggressive move in 1 direction to beat time decay, 2 out of the 3 outcomes (flat or opposite direction) directly profit the option seller. Statistically, the seller starts with a 66.6% edge before any technical analysis.

🎯

2. The Delta Sweet Spot (0.15 - 0.25)

An option's Delta is a direct proxy for its probability of expiring In-The-Money (ITM). Selling strikes with Delta between 0.15 and 0.25 guarantees an 75% to 85% statistical probability of expiring worthless. It avoids deep OTM options (too little premium) and ATM options (excessive directional risk).

3. The Short Gamma Trap

ATM options have the highest Gamma, and Gamma peaks near expiry. Shorting naked ATM options means being "Short Gamma": if the underlying swings, Delta accelerates violently against you, causing exponential losses. "Option writers eat like a chicken but shit like an elephant" if they short naked ATM. Always sell OTM outside the 1-SD band!
